This Callout runs for several lines on purpose. Its job is to show what a filled
surface does when it stops being an aside and starts being a section: whether the
inset ring still reads at this height, whether the icon column looks abandoned
next to a block of text, and whether the reader's eye can still find the prose
that follows it. It carries a [link](https://nextjs.org/docs), some `inline
